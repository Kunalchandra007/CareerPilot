CareerPilot

Your job search, run from your own machine.

A local-first AI dashboard that evaluates job listings against your CV, scans portals, and tracks every application — with your data never leaving your computer.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

Free · open source · runs entirely offline on your machine · requires Node.js 18+

🔒 Private by design

Your CV, applications, and reports live only on your disk. No account, no cloud, no server sees your data.

🎯 Fit, not spam

Scores each listing against your real experience and steers you toward the few roles worth your time.

🛰️ Zero-cost scanning

Pulls fresh roles straight from Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever APIs — no API key, no tokens, no bill.

Get started in 4 steps

  1. Install Node.js LTS (one-time).
  2. Download the project (button above) and unzip it anywhere, e.g. C:\CareerPilot.
  3. Double-click CareerPilot.bat.
  4. First run builds the app, then opens http://localhost:3000. The setup wizard walks you through adding your CV and profile.
Why is there no “try it online” button? CareerPilot reads and writes files on your machine and runs its scanner locally — that's what keeps your data private. It's meant to be downloaded and run, not hosted. This page is just the download.
